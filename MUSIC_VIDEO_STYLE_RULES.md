# Music Video Style Rules (memory bank)

Locked creative rules for all future music videos in this project.

## 1. One style per video (do not mix within a song)

When a style is chosen for a track — pop art, digital art, anime, oil, watercolor, sketch, charcoal, etc. — **that style stays consistent for every scene of that video**.

- Rotating styles applies **across different songs/videos**, not inside one video.
- Do **not** switch art mediums or visual themes mid-song.

## 2. House “genre” (always true)

Think of this like a music genre: songs can differ, but they stay in-family.

**Always present:**

- **Thick black outlines / thick line work**
- **Pop-art / anime / digital-art energy** as the house language
- **Underlying tone = Luis Royo–like fantasy art DNA**  
  (painterly fantasy figure work — sensual/dramatic fantasy illustration feel — as the quiet through-line under whatever top medium is chosen)
- **Cyberpunk / futuristic / emo / digital-fantasy** atmosphere in the world

“Underlying tone” means it can read as background DNA or a measured important undercurrent — not a second competing style layered on top.

## 3. Per-video top layer

For each new song, pick **one** cohesive top treatment within the house genre, e.g.:

- thick-line pop art
- thick-line anime
- thick-line digital painting
- oil / watercolor / pencil / charcoal **with thick black line structure**

Stay on that choice for the whole track.

## 4. Hero / main character

- User-supplied photos are **likeness references only**.
- Never use the reference photo as a still in the video.
- Never copy the exact photo composition (e.g. selfie / peace-sign pose) as a frame.
- Render the hero **in the locked style of that video**, matching identity (hair, face, vibe) not the source image file.

## 5. Starring mix (from prior direction)

- About **~35%** clear starring hero frames.
- Other scenes: story/world; some may include her without starring.
- Do not put her in every shot.

## 6. Motion

- Prefer varied pans / zooms / punches synced to music intensity.
- Use camera shake **sparingly**.
- Prefer animated scene clips (Seedance / image-to-video) over still slideshows when API access allows.

## Reference artist

- **Luis Royo** (fantasy illustration/painting) = underlying visual genre for the house look.
